### Changed

- Read a whole Needs you message before acting on it: a question or report
  longer than three lines shows **More**, and a tap (or **More**) shows all of
  it in place with its links; **Less** shortens it again. Keyboard focus
  entering a clipped message reveals its links. The agent's name now
  opens its Chat on every item, as **Chat about this** does. Every Needs you
  item ends in the same bottom row: **Dismiss** or **Mark reviewed** on the
  left, **Chat about this** or **Follow up…** on the right, with **Answer**
  beside the answer field.
- On a phone outside Zen, **Needs you** in the top bar is an icon with a count
  badge right after the view's name, like the bar's other icons.
