### Changed

- Open a herdr-core terminal in World's own window: move, resize, minimize,
  restore, arrange and switch it like a Herdr terminal on desktop and phone.
  Resizing only rescales the drawing until you type; your next key sends the
  window's new size. Several core terminals can be open at once.
- Open a herdr-core agent in its Inspector window from Town, Board, Desk,
  Needs you, the agent menu or a phone's switch list, with Chat, Files and
  Changes tabs. Chat has **Watch terminal** to open its terminal window.

### Fixed

- Stop sending Herdr-only requests (quests, Herdr transcripts, step reads) to
  a herdr-core connection; the page now refuses them before they leave.
