### Changed

- A herdr-core agent has one pin, shared with Herdr's apps: pinning its Town
  desk, Board card or peek in World pins it in the apps, and a pin made or
  removed in an app shows on its desk and card within seconds. World still
  decides where a pinned desk or card stands. Pinned agents come first in a
  phone's switch list. Herdr (TUI) agents keep World's own pin.

### Removed

- The separate favourite star in a core agent's peek: its pin replaces it.

### Fixed

- A core agent opened from Town, Board, Desk or Needs you with Agents open set
  to Chat now turns to Chat once its conversation reads, instead of staying
  on Terminal.
