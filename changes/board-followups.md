- Board's Next control opens the next agent who needs you. Its menu now offers
  Whiteboard and Blackboard surfaces, separate status colours and Help.
- Drag a pane row onto another tab on the same host to move just that pane,
  including with touch hold; cancelling sends no move, and closing the old room
  requires confirmation.

  including the confirmation before closing its old room.
- Batch dense rooms' overview squares to keep terminal input responsive at
  maximum scale, preserving each pane's status and the status-pulse controls.
