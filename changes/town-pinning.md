  the window header on a phone) or a card on
  Board (**Pin** in its bar, or the pin in its terminal's header on a phone).

  growth, Tidy and settling, and a pinned desk stays in a room that has more
  tabs than Town shows. Agents at a pinned
  desk never go to the lounge: they visit reception only when they need you or
  finish, and walk straight back once seen. Pins are saved on the World
  service, so every device sees them.
