### Changed

- Pinned desks in Town reorder on their room's rug, as pinned browser tabs do:
  drag a pinned desk onto another pinned desk (or press **M** on it) and it
  takes that place. Dropped anywhere else it glides back, with the hint
  "Unpin to move it out of the front row"; a drag never unpins it. The order
  is saved as World's own Town layout, so a reload and every device keep it,
  and a desk pinned later joins the end.
