### Added

- Put an agent in the bin from its window's **⋯** menu in Town, Board or Desk.
  In Town it walks to the bus stop nearest its room; a bus comes every three
  minutes, picks up everyone waiting and closes their agents once it has left
  the map. The **Bin** tray shows who is waiting and the time to the next bus,
  with **Put back** and **Close now**. A pinned desk or tab keeps its last agent, and
  restarting World closes nothing that was waiting.
