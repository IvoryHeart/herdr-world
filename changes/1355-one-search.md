### Changed

- `/`, Ctrl+F and Cmd+K now open the same search box in every view, as do a
  view's search button and the phone's Find or act. Choosing an agent goes to
  it in the view you are in (Town flies to its table); Shift+Enter or the
  row's Open terminal opens its terminal. Commands such as new workspace stay
  in the same box.
- Desk's lane search is now named Filter.

### Removed

- The separate search boxes and results strip in Town, Board and Desk.
