### Added

- Seen and favourite are shared with Herdr's apps for herdr-core agents.
  Looking at a core agent's peek in Town or Board, on a page in front of you,
  clears its Done in Herdr's phone and desktop apps; marking it unread in an
  app keeps it unread in World. The star in the peek is Herdr's favourite
  (`herdr.pinned`). World's desk and card pins and Mark reviewed stay World's
  own.
- Core agents send Web Push notifications when they finish a turn or start
  needing you, from core's own status events.
