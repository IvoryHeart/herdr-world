### Changed

- Town rooms extend to fit: a room grows to eight tables wide and then adds
  rows, up to 127 tables, and its building grows around it, so a room of
  agents spawning agents shows every table (rooms used to stop at 19, hiding
  the newest tabs). A room with even more hides idle tables first and keeps
  ones that need you, are working or are new. Zooming into a very large room
  opens at its first tables.
