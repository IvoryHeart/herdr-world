### Changed

- One **Group runtimes** setting, under Settings → This World, now groups a
  machine's Herdr TUI and herdr-core the same way in Town, the navigator and
  Desk, and is shared by every browser. With **One per machine** (the
  default) the navigator shows the machine once with a row per runtime.
  It replaces Town's own Town buildings setting; a browser's earlier choice
  carries over.
