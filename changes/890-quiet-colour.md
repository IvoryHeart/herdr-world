### Changed

- Colour is now quiet identity and status the only loud colour. The terminal
  colour palette is eight muted tones of equal lightness, so an agent's status
  mark stands out in Town, Spaces and Board. A tab without a colour of its own
  shows its room's colour, so a room without chosen colours reads as one colour
  instead of eight scattered ones; rooms still differ by default.
- A desk moved to another room keeps only a colour picked for it; otherwise it
  takes its new room's colour.
