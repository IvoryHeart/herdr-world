### Changed

- Town, Board, Desk, Chat and the phone bar use one set of agent marks and
  words: • Working, ! Needs you, ? Asks you, ✓ Done, – Idle or Reviewed,
  ~ No signal and × Offline. Board no longer marks an agent with no signal
  with **?**, which now always means an owner question.
- Reviewed is one record on the service: marking finished work reviewed in
  Town, Desk or Needs you clears it in every view and browser. Town no longer
  keeps its own reviewed marks in the browser.
- One vocabulary for the same things: Town's Summary says **Done, not
  reviewed** and **needs you**, Desk names an agent's **desk** rather than its
  tab, and Board calls a pane without an agent a **terminal**, as Town does.

### Added

- **Needs you** is one movable window in every view, opened from the
  **Needs you** counter beside the view buttons. Questions agents ask, agents
  blocked on their own question and finished work to review are messages from
  their agents, with Chat's formatting and answers in place: options or a
  typed answer, **Dismiss**, the agent's own question, **Mark reviewed** and
  **Open agent**. Town's reception letters, Desk's **Answer** and a Board
  card's **!**/**✓** badge open it at that item.

### Removed

- Town's Needs you rail. Its questions and waiting agents are in the Needs you
  window; Summary still lists them.
