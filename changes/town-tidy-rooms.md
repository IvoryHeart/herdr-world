### Added

- Tidy rooms in Town: the sparkles button on a building's reception board
  glides all of that building's rooms into neat, gap-free columns in Herdr's
  workspace order, the arrangement a never-moved building has. World saves it
  like a room move, so it stays after a reload and Spaces keeps the order;
  tidying again changes nothing, and Undo in the note puts the rooms back.
