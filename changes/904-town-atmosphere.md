- Town has depth that follows the time of day: buildings cast sun shadows
  that move with the hour, cloud shadows drift by day, street lamps glow at
  night and fireflies come out over the grass. It stays under the status
  light and never shades a room or desk; with calm or reduced motion nothing
  drifts. Rugs are woven with a bound edge, and room walls shade the floor
  beneath them.
