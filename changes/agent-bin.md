### Added

- Close agents with a bin: drop an agent in it and it closes when its 30-second
  countdown ends, unless you take it back first. Board, Desk and Town share one
  countdown and one tray (Put back, Close now, Resume). Town's bin is its bus
  stops: one red double-decker stops at every stop on a fixed timetable, picks
  up the agents waiting there and waits at the last stop until 0:00 (going
  round again for an agent dropped at a stop it has passed), then drives off
  the map, and the agent closes only when it has left. A drop
  before it pulls away restarts the shared countdown. Nothing closes before
  then or before you choose Close now, and a World restart cancels every
  countdown without closing anything.
