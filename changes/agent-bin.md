### Added

- Close an agent by dropping its Board card in the bin, or by sending it to
  Town's London bus stop. Nothing closes until the countdown ends (30 seconds by
  default; **Menu → Bin countdown** offers 10 s to 2 min), and the countdown
  carries on if you reload. Open the bin, or press and hold the bus, to pause it
  and take the agent back out. Pinned tabs and unreachable hosts are refused.
