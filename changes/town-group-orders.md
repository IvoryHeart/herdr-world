### Added

- Select several agents in Town and give them one order: Shift+drag a box or
  Shift+click on a desktop, or **Select** on an agent's peek or a table's card
  on a phone. The Town board sign becomes a command card that counts the
  selection by state and offers **Mark reviewed (N)**, **Send a message (N of
  M)** with each agent's result (sent, queued or refused, and who was left
  out and why), **Show them** and **Clear**.
