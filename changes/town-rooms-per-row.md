### Added

- Settings → Town & views → Rooms per row: Auto (the default) keeps each Town
  building about square, a little wider than tall, so a machine with many rooms
  is no longer a tall two-column tower; 2, 3, 4 or 5 sets every building and
  applies at once. Phones keep three compact rooms per row on Auto.

### Fixed

- A Town building with one moved room now fills its rooms per row with the
  others, instead of squeezing every unmoved room into that room's width.
