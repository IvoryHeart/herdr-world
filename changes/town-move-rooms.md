### Added

- Move whole rooms in Town: drag a room by its name or wall (touch and hold on
  a phone) onto another room of its building, or use the room card's Move
  before and Move after arrows. Its tables and agents go with it, outlines
  show where every room will stand, and Escape puts it back. The order is
  Herdr's workspace order, so Spaces shows the same and a reload keeps it.
- Escape puts back any desk, agent or room being carried in Town.
