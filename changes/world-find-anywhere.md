### Changed

- Cmd+K (Ctrl+K) finds agents on every host and runtime from every view,
  Spaces and the phone's Find or act included, by name, room, machine,
  runtime, harness, status or task. Enter opens the agent's terminal on its
  own connection; in Town, Board and Desk, Shift+Enter or the row's Show
  button selects it there instead. Agents on offline hosts stay listed and
  say why they can't open.
