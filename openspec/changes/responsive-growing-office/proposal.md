## Why

Phone Office opens onto decorative CEO space and requires sideways navigation to
compare host receptions. Its eight-desk presentation limit also hides part of a
workspace instead of letting its room grow with the observed tabs and agents.

## What Changes

- Deliver Office before the separate, deferred
  [Graph exploration](../elastic-graph-exploration/proposal.md). The owner selected
  vertical scrolling after comparing interactive vertical and horizontal studies.
- On phones, use one vertical Office flow: receptions needing attention first,
  then Economy, Workforce and workspace rooms. Neither receptions nor workspace rooms
  require horizontal swiping. Remove the decorative CEO room from the phone
  composition; retain its useful status information and access to idle agents.
- Keep the pixel scene, reception tables, characters, real workspace rooms and
  tab desks. Preserve the desktop CEO/reception/Agent Bar composition while making
  desktop rooms grow with their own contents. Desktop desk grids adapt to width
  and content; smaller rooms fill space alongside larger rooms without shared
  tallest-room row gaps.
- Replace scene-owned room, tab and room-agent totals with complete logical
  layout over the admitted observation. More than eight tabs, sixteen room agents
  or 128 rooms must remain reachable without raising one fixed cap to another.
  Rendering and semantic-control admission remain bounded by the viewport.
- Give each host a pixel-art building with a stepped roof, brickwork and a
  wooden host sign containing its truthful summary.
  Use the same packing for rooms inside buildings and buildings across desktop
  width; stack buildings on phones. Host collapse preserves individual room
  choices and native work, while navigation reopens the exact necessary host.
  Occupied buildings share usable screen width and reflow their rooms together;
  joint room-shape/gap packing, carpeted interiors, wood reception/lounge floors and exterior asphalt roads
  distinguish the spaces while retaining the pixel-art palette.
- Reuse the existing shell search for reachable room/agent navigation and keep
  room collapse/expand, removing the separate navigation row so a large room does
  not force the user to scroll through every desk to reach later workspaces.
- Preserve qualified selection, exact pane actions, shared Inspector ownership,
  creation readiness, truthful stale states and optional metric availability.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `world-surfaces`: responsive Office composition, content-sized rooms, complete
  observed item reachability, viewport rendering, room navigation and creation.

## Impact

Browser Office projection, geometry, layout, drawing, visible-item admission,
semantic targets, scrolling preferences and focused tests. Update the Office
contracts and knowledge routes when implementation is delivered. No service RPC,
runtime identity, dependency or distribution change is planned.

The responsive Office layout and host buildings are implemented in this candidate
and synchronized into current specs. Tree navigation and Graph appearance, physics
and gestures are outside Office delivery. The original interactive study is
evidence for the selected scroll direction, not production renderer or performance
acceptance.

The later host-focus and playful-arrangement discussion is recorded as a proposed
interaction study in [the design](design.md#interaction-study-host-focus-and-arranging-proposed-not-implemented).
It does not authorize those interactions as part of the implemented Office scope.

The owner clarified the proposal: buildings/receptions reorder hosts visually;
rooms reorder Herdr workspaces; desks reorder tabs or transfer them between
workspaces on the same host; agents remain attached to their desks/screens/panes.
The design records existing reorder paths and the cross-workspace transfer
capability investigation still required before implementation.
