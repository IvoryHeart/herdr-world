## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Apartments entry points

Town's green APARTMENTS sign on its sandstone park building, the town center
ring's Buildings and Board's hub SHALL open the same residents. The building's
sign and Board's hub SHALL show resident counts; the ring SHALL NOT. Town SHALL
place the building beside the town center, left of the plaza or right when
needed, and hide it when the town is too small. Bus arrivals SHALL increase the
sign's count as their agents walk in.

#### Scenario: Open from Board on a phone

- **WHEN** the user opens Apartments from Board's hub
- **THEN** the same residents are available as through Town's building and ring
