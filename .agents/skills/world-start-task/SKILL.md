Treat the short goal as the task. Read AGENTS.md and the
[start/continue procedure](../../../docs/agent-development.md#start-and-continue).
Inspect the existing task record before choosing a start, resume or recovery command.
Use the requested parent PR, report the printed worktree/job/run IDs, and relay managed
intake questions. Preserve the owner's constraints and prior authorization.

The coordinator launches the harness before doing feature research or implementation.
A supervisor-assigned worker follows its current phase instead. Do not duplicate the
inner lead's planning or relay routine pair handoffs. For periodic updates or context
recovery use world-recap-run; its factual recap does not require a monitoring agent.

Read only the applicable procedure:

- [Reference images and startup](../../../docs/agent-development.md#start-and-continue)
- [Changed controls on a stopped task](../../../docs/agent-development.md#upgrade-a-stopped-task-to-new-controls)
- [Background supervision](../../../docs/agent-development.md#background-supervision)
- [Delivery and evidence](../../../docs/agent-development.md#delivery-and-evaluation)

A failed or exhausted run remains unfinished. Do not silently finish it interactively,
restart it to reset limits, or count interactive maintenance as a successful trial.
