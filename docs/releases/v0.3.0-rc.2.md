# Herdr World 0.3.0-rc.2 release candidate

This candidate keeps Chat usable when an agent changes its worktree and makes
it easier to learn how World fits into your day. The highlights below explain
what changed since rc.1, then what 0.3.0 brings to your workspace overall.

Test the candidate across your devices before stable 0.3.0; corrections after
publication receive a new RC number.

## Changed since rc.1

- **Keep chatting while an agent changes its worktree.** Chat no longer freezes
  when an agent moves to another folder or branch. Keep reading the conversation
  and sending messages while Files, Changes and Commits wait for the worktree
  to be checked again.
- **See how World helps you manage a busy day.** The refreshed website and the
  new **Promoted to CEO** film show how to find waiting agents, follow progress
  and review finished work across Town, Board, Desk and Spaces.
- **Get started with a task you can follow.** The rewritten
  [tutorial](https://github.com/IvoryHeart/herdr-world/blob/v0.3.0-rc.2/docs/TUTORIAL.md)
  starts in your own project: give an agent a small README task, follow its
  progress, review its changes and pick up the workspace from your phone.

The **Needs you** count on the browser tab and app icon, and **Fit right** /
**Fit left**, were already included in the published rc.1. They are part of
0.3.0's highlights below.

## What 0.3.0 brings overall

World helps you find work, spot agents that need you and keep track of progress
across a busy workspace. Town, Board and Desk share attention and review state,
while better search, usage displays and phone controls make it easier to decide
where to spend your time. These are the highlights since World 0.2.7.

### Find the right work faster

- **Search or act from one box.** Press **Ctrl+K** (**Cmd+K** on Mac) to find
  buildings, rooms, tables, agents and terminals, see their status and location,
  switch views or run an Actions menu command. In Zen, click **⌘** beside the
  view buttons; on a phone, open the corner view menu and choose **Find or act**.
  In Chat, terminals and text fields, use **Ctrl+Alt+K** (**Cmd+Option+K** on
  Mac). Saved shortcut presets retain their keys.
- **Search a busy Town without losing your bearings.** Search matches words in
  any order across Town, Board and Desk. Town highlights matches and takes you
  to a chosen result. On desktop, open search with **/** or **Ctrl+F**
  (**Cmd+F** on Mac). Lenses colour Town and Board by idle time, spend, pull
  requests, quests or agent tool. Search highlights stay visible over the lens,
  and closing search keeps the lens on. Town's **+** zoom button also works
  reliably when the town is barely wider than your desktop window.
- **Keep pull requests in sight.** The post office beside Town center collects
  your open pull requests, even after their agents have gone, with ready-to-merge
  parcels first.
- **Find work across servers.** Spaces adds an **All servers** list, cross-server
  Pane Search and richer tab chips, so you can locate a session without opening
  each server in turn.

### See where your attention will help

- **One Needs you tab across every view.** Questions, blocked agents and finished
  work appear together. Answer quickly, attach files or screenshots, open Chat,
  dismiss an item or mark work reviewed. Review marks follow you across views
  and browsers. Off-screen arrows and **N** / **Shift+N** take you through the
  items that need you. The Town Master opens each visit with **Since you left**;
  you can turn that greeting off in your browser.
- **Know when to return to World.** The browser tab and its icon show the
  **Needs you** count even while the tab is in the background. An installed
  World also shows the count on its app icon where supported. Use **Menu →
  Count on tab and app icon** to turn it off for this browser.
- **See progress without opening every agent.** Working agents show their current
  step in thought bubbles, with more detail as you zoom in. An unusually long
  step turns amber. Board cards also show working steps, review state and idle
  time, so you can check progress from either view.
- **Plan around remaining capacity.** Town's Fuel board shows Codex and Claude
  subscription windows, percent left, reset times and how old each reading is.
  Claude sessions launched from World report limits automatically; installing
  the Claude World skill also enables readings from sessions started elsewhere.
  Agents stopped by a Codex or Claude usage limit show an empty fuel can and a
  reset clock, clearing when the reset passes or work resumes.
- **Understand usage and expenses.** The City expenses billboard and each
  building's reception show usage for Claude Code, Codex, Pi and Gemini, with
  optional local price estimates. Agent peeks and windows show context fill
  where available, with a sweat drop at 85% or more. Missing, stale, partial or
  unpriced readings are labelled so you can judge what the figures tell you.

### Organize agents and help them move forward

- **A clearer Town to work in.** Woods, gardens, ponds and lanes surround the
  buildings. Town center opens common actions, reception is upstairs and the
  lounge downstairs. Each building has a fixed **+ Add room** doorway. Move and
  tidy rooms, filter which agents appear at reception, and inspect room and
  table cards. New agents arrive on foot, and moving between rooms and hosts
  keeps your place with less redraw.
- **Manage work directly from Board.** Its summaries, host zones and rooms share
  Town's counts, expenses and limits. Rename, close or reorder rooms and tabs,
  peek at an agent, answer a question and mark finished work reviewed without
  switching views.
- **Clear finished agents with a chance to undo.** Drag an agent onto Town or
  Board's bin, a Town bus stop, or use its window menu. Town's bus collects
  waiting agents on its four-minute route and closes them after leaving the
  screen. The bin tray offers **Put back** and **Close now**; restarting World
  does not close waiting agents.
- **Teach agents how to work with World.** In **Menu → Configuration →
  Integrations**, the **World skill** controls let you install, update or remove
  guidance for asking questions, saving results and reporting task summaries
  and parent-child relationships. Start a new agent session after installation.
  Codex, Pi and Kimi share one skill file; removal names every affected tool.
  The same controls are available through `herdr-world integration install <harness>`,
  `herdr-world integration status [harness]` and
  `herdr-world integration uninstall <harness>`. See the
  [World skill guide](https://github.com/IvoryHeart/herdr-world/blob/v0.3.0-rc.2/docs/harness-integrations.md).
- **Answer more questions in Chat.** Respond to supported Claude forms and trust
  questions, Codex choices and supported command menus. Read complete questions
  or switch to Terminal when Chat cannot offer an answer control. Saved answers
  have an Outbox where you can inspect delivery and recover from problems;
  uncertain sends are not automatically repeated.

### Keep working across devices and visits

- **More comfortable phone controls.** Minimize agent and terminal windows and
  restore them from a chip. Town's room controls have larger touch targets, and
  opening Chat no longer resizes a terminal you cannot see. Chat source links
  open in the same window.
- **Keep a window beside your work.** On desktop, choose **⋯ → Fit right** or
  **Fit left** to dock an agent or terminal window at a comfortable width in
  Town, Board, Desk or Spaces. It remembers the width you last dragged a docked
  window to, so you can keep Chat in sight without giving it half the screen.
- **More ways to work with sessions and files.** Mobile shells can launch saved
  agent commands. History Chat adds terminal input, image uploads and speech.
  PDF previews work even when the browser has no native PDF viewer. Configure
  worktree placement and host commands to suit your workspace; Ranger adds
  broader task scheduling and support for the service account's Claude CLI.
- **Your workspace remembers more.** Questions, answers, results, connection
  profiles, chimes and Board state survive restarts. Terminal appearance,
  review marks and more settings follow the World service across browsers.
  Existing saved data is imported with private backups.

## Install or upgrade

**Already on rc.1?** Back up your private World data as below, then install
0.3.0-rc.2 using the same channel. The distribution channels and platform support
are unchanged; there is no additional migration specific to rc.2.

**Coming from World 0.2.7?** The 0.3.0 data migration and rollback precautions
below still apply.

Before upgrading, stop your World service and back up its entire private data
directory, including SQLite WAL files if present. Back up an overridden legacy
ledger separately. Preserve configuration, connections, secrets and browser
storage. Restart only the World instance being upgraded.

World imports existing JSON into its private `world.sqlite` and retains private
backups. Versions predating SQLite cannot see new database answers or edits.
To roll back, stop World and restore a matching whole-directory backup; do not
copy only the database or mix WAL files. Some older completion marks may need
review again.
See the
[backup and recovery guide](https://github.com/IvoryHeart/herdr-world/blob/v0.3.0-rc.2/docs/DEPLOYMENT.md#durable-questions-results-and-backups).

After this candidate is published, install its exact version with:

```bash
npm install --global @ivoryheart/herdr-world@0.3.0-rc.2
```

Alternatively, use its platform archive, the Unix installer with
`HERDR_WORLD_VERSION=0.3.0-rc.2`, or the separate
`IvoryHeart/tap/herdr-world-rc` Formula after its tap PR is reviewed and merged.
Archives are available for Linux, macOS and Windows on x64 and ARM64. npm needs
Node.js 22.14.0 or newer. Source builds require Bun 1.4.4 or newer; installed
archives and plugins need no external Bun.

This is a prerelease: it uses npm's `next` tag and the separate Homebrew RC
Formula, leaving stable update channels unchanged. Before merge, the release
PR's `release-preview` label requests platform archives, npm packages and a
Homebrew preview from CI. Download them from the successful run's **Artifacts**
section and follow the
[pre-merge preview instructions](https://github.com/IvoryHeart/herdr-world/blob/v0.3.0-rc.2/docs/release.md#pre-merge-platform-previews).
Preview binaries identify themselves as `0.0.0-rc.<CI run number>`.
RC publication does not deploy the refreshed public website; stable publication
includes that deployment.

## Practical limits

- **Check uncertain answers in the Outbox.** Automatic saved-answer delivery is
  available only for newly verified local Linux command-line agents in supported
  Chat sessions. After a World restart, saved answers remain available but old
  sends cannot automatically resume. Other answers can be retrieved with
  `herdr-world ask get`.
- **Treat usage displays as observations.** Subscription gauges are not live
  billing guarantees. Claude's first reading may take about a minute after an
  answer, and project-specific status lines can prevent readings. Expense
  estimates need a local price table. City and building boards share UTC totals;
  hover or tap for caveats. Unknown context capacity shows no bar rather than
  zero usage.
- **Some remote controls remain unavailable.** On remote Windows connections,
  file tools, Git tools and agent readiness checks are unavailable. Manage connections
  has no remote upgrade control, and device-facing forwarding controls are not
  included.
- **World skill controls apply to the local connection.** SSH installation and
  automatic skill upgrades are not included. Set up the agent tool before
  installing its skill. Damaged Claude settings or recovery files must be
  repaired before installation can proceed safely.
- **Work-item quests and macOS signing and notarization remain open.**
