Chrome

One bar per window

Town window (v4 skin)

  • ‹ Back · avatar (its ring is the terminal colour) · name + status pill, with a location line underneath (room · desk · branch). Tap the location line for branch, worktree, path and PR.
  • One top selector with Chat, Terminal, Changes and Files sits in the same bar on every screen size. The chips are icons until the window is 640px wide; Changes shows its count.
  • Next › is the primary action on desktop and turns gold when someone else needs you. On a phone it moves into ⋯, because the need-you pill already covers it.
  • ⋯ holds the rare actions: snap, float or dock, maximise, minimise, colour and pop-out. Double-click the bar to maximise; drag it to the edges to snap.
  • Pane chips get a second row only when the desk has 2 or more panes. At the bottom there is only Chat's composer (attach, reply box, send), and only in Chat. Terminal, Changes and Files have no bottom bar.

Board and Spaces (calm skin, same frame)

  • A 38px bar holding the colour swatch, name, location line, view chips, then ⋯, maximise and close. Minimise and snap move into ⋯.
  • The PR card is no longer a 58px band above Changes; PR status lives in the location popover and as a pill in the Changes toolbar.
  • The repository icon, the second maximise button and the separate colour button are dropped. Each existed twice.
  • Spaces merges the app bar and the tab strip into one 40px title bar with the tabs inside it, the way VS Code, Warp and Zed do.

Town shell

  • The app top bar, tab strip and sidebar are off by default in Town. The canvas runs full-bleed under a prototype-style HUD bar: brand, counts, a need you pill, Jump to… /, view, hosts and menu.
  • The ☰ on the brand opens Workspaces as a drawer over the map, and the choice is remembered per view. Zen (Ctrl+Alt+Z) in Town hides the HUD down to the need-you pill.
  • The Needs-you tray is a left rail at 900px and up, and a bottom strip below that.
  • Camera: a vertical +, −, ALL cluster on desktop that moves out of the dock's way. On phone it becomes one 44px fit button: tap fits the town, a long press opens − and +, and pinch zooms.

Prior art used

  • VS Code, Zed and Warp put tabs in the title bar, which is the model for the Spaces merged bar. VS Code's breadcrumb, shown on demand, is the model for the location line and its popover.
  • iTerm2 compact and minimal themes drop the title bar into the tab row. Window actions go in a menu and maximise is a double-click.
  • claude.ai and ChatGPT use one slim header with the content first and the composer at the bottom. The Town window follows that layout, and Chat owns its contents.
  • The Town v4 prototype supplies the docked terminal header (‹, name, where, Next ›), the camera cluster, the Needs-you tray, chunky 40–44px targets and the panel material.
