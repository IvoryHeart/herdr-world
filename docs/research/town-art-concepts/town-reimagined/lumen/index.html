Lumen

A town of glass and light floating in the dark. You can see into every building, and every agent is a small being of light.

The town sits on a round mirror disc in the void, like a spatial widget you could set on your desk. Buildings are clear glass, so every room in a host's tower is visible at once. A busy floor glows in its project's colour and an empty one is just an outline. Agents are wisps whose state is how they glow: a steady pulse when working, orbiting sparks when thinking, and a column of amber light when they need you, so a question is visible from across town. The light-packets on the roads are traffic. The bus is a maglev circling the town hall. Releases leave port under sails of light. Questions fly as amber arcs to a post office crowned with orbiting envelopes. This is the material language of 2026: frosted glass, depth, emissive light, dark-mode native. It doubles as a dashboard, because brightness means activity and you read it the way you read a city from a plane at night.

The whole town

The town hall ziggurat is the hub, ringed by the maglev loop, with its live counters floating above. ATLAS and NIMBUS are glass towers with one floor per room. Around the hub sit the post office (top), the lounge and apartment pods (left), the memory library and the PR warehouse (front), and the sea with one release lane per project (right). A new host, pine, is being printed by a beam from the sky.

The cast

A teardrop of light with a visor. The harness shows in the colour and the halo: Claude has a ring of sparks, Codex a diamond, Gemini twin orbiting stars, and Rex a chevron. Each state is a different kind of light, so you can read it from far away.

Close-ups

Glass means no hidden rooms. A busy floor glows and has floating code panes, and an empty floor is an outline. Reception is a glass counter with light pads, where the queue stands oldest first.

Night, in the rain

Rain falls as light streaks and rings spread across the mirror floor. Walkers carry a small dome of light, and the beacons cut through the weather.
