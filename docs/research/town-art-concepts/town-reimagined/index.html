Town, reimagined · three 2.5D art directions

Three towns.

The same crew, the same buildings and the same states, drawn as three very different worlds.

Each direction covers the whole cast: agents in every state, rooms lit and dark, reception, the lounge, the town hall, the post office, apartments, the library, the shipyard with a lane and ship per project and PRs as crates, roads and the bus, day and night, rain and snow, a new host arriving, and how it reads on a phone. Everything is live and drawn in code. No images are generated, and every scene animates.

How they differ

Pop-Up: you look down into itIsometric. Rooms are open-top trays, so you see desks from above. Character comes from the material: paper, shadow and grain.
Lumen: you look through itIsometric glass. Every floor is visible through the walls, and state is light. It doubles as an ambient dashboard and is at its best at night.
Cutaway: you look across itSide-on with parallax depth. Every room is visible at once with words in balloons, and on a phone it becomes a sideways strip.
